SPOILER WARNING! Skip to the 2nd section if you dont want to spoil the game The Witness

The Witness is a game about understanding the universe. A quest to understand your surroundings and to follow knowledge like a sinking star. The game takes place on an island, and it can be divided into 3 categories:

  1. Puzzles on LCD: These are right in front of you to solve.
  2. Environmental puzzles: These puzzles are present in the environment, and the game doesn't point them out. You are supposed to observe your surroundings and figure out the correct perspective to see the environment and solve them.
  3. "Easter eggs" in the environment: These are not meant to be solved. They are just meant to be observed.

The game has no reward system. You have to be intrinsically motivated to understand the island. Other than the puzzles on the LCD, the other 2 can easily be missed if you are not meditating and observing your environment. In fact, you can complete the game without noticing the existence of the environmental puzzles or easter eggs. This is a metaphor for the quest for knowledge. You can do surface-level work(puzzles on the LCD) your entire life and miss out on the deep and rich world that hides underneath it(environmental puzzles and easter eggs). There is no reward or achievement to gain by venturing deep. It is done for the sake of it, for the joy of understanding and discovering. One has to view an idea from multiple perspectives to understand it, and the witness does an excellent job of communicating this to the player, as games are one of the best media for communicating such ideas.

Section 2

I lived in C. V. Raman Block for 2 years while in college, and the rest of the essay will be on how beautifully it is designed.

The temperature would reach ~40C, and the block was cleverly designed to keep our rooms cool. There were rooms only in the north and south, and the east and west only had washrooms. This meant that other than the top floor and rooms in the corner, no room would receive direct sunlight.

If you go down the walkway, you will notice that all rooms are on one side and all rooms face the centre of the block, which is a spacious area with badminton courts, benches, etc. This improves the airflow, along with these "walls with holes" that are found near the washroom(east and west perimeter of the block). This area cannot flood the ground floor walkway when it rains because there is a drainage that runs around the perimeter of the area.

There are 2 power lines for each room. One powers the lights, and the other powers the sockets. There is a switch outside the room that can be turned off when you are leaving the room to turn off all lights(1st power line), while the other power line continues charging your devices.

Outside the block, there is a garden with a tree where you can sit in its shade, as there are seats placed underneath it. On entering the block, there are 2 staircases, one for the east and the other for the west side.
